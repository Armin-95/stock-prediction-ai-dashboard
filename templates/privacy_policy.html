5. Cookies

This website uses necessary cookies and technical data to make the website work correctly, securely, and reliably.

Necessary cookies may be used without optional consent because they are required for basic website functionality, security, session handling, rate limiting, or remembering your cookie choice.

Optional cookies are only used after you give consent. Optional cookies may be used to remember preferences and save AI chat history on the prediction page.
